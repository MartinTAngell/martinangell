## Extended Summary
[LearnSPH](https://learnsph.com) is an educational project meant to ease the learning curve for the techniques and mathematics behind Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) simulations. Through short lessons, quizzes, and interactive visualizations, we make difficult-to-grasp topics easier.

We cover simple and advanced topics ranging from kernel functions and basic SPH forces to pressure solvers, with more lessons and visualizers to come in the future.
